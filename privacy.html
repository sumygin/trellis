Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trellis handles information when you use the Trellis collaborative board application.

Information we collect

When you create or sign in to an account, Trellis may receive your email address and account identifier from Supabase Authentication. If you use Google sign-in, Google may provide your name, email address, profile image, and account identifier according to your Google account settings.

Trellis stores the boards, cards, lists, invitations, permissions, descriptions, links, and other content that you create or that other board members add.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, provide board collaboration, enforce board permissions, synchronize changes, respond to invitations, and maintain the service.

Service providers

Trellis uses Supabase for authentication, database storage, and realtime synchronization, and Vercel for hosting. These providers process information to provide their services. Their own privacy policies may also apply.

Sharing

Board content is shared with members and invitees who have access to that board. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the service and its users.

Security and retention

Access controls and database policies are used to protect board data. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained while it is needed to provide the service or until you request deletion, subject to legal and operational requirements.

Your choices

You can stop using Trellis, manage your Google account permissions, or request deletion of your account and associated data. For privacy requests, contact the service operator through the project repository.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above indicates when it was last revised.
